What do these channels mean?
Reward
Anticipation / curiosity (nucleus accumbens). Driven by hooks, open loops, novelty and build-up to a payoff. High reward = viewers want to see what happens next.
Attention
Visual salience that holds the eye. Driven by motion, cut rate, face size, contrast and on-screen text. This is what wins the first-3s hold.
Emotion
Emotional arousal (amygdala/insula). Driven by audio energy, vocal pitch/pace, expression intensity and sentiment. High arousal predicts sharing.
Aversion
Scroll-away / drop-off risk (anterior insula). Raised by dead air, slow starts, low motion, monotone and over-long shots. Lower is better.
Language
Meaning & relatability (MPFC/language areas). Driven by self-reference, clarity, a clear message/CTA and novelty, which drive comments and sharing intent.
These are a research-informed simulation of neural response, not a real brain scan.
